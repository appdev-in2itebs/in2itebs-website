# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Enterprise technology and procurement decision-makers evaluating In2IT EBS's platform, advisory, engineering and managed delivery capabilities. Secondary users are partners and prospective employees. Source: existing project brief, content plans and owner feedback.

## Product Purpose

Make the real service scope and delivery evidence easy to understand and help qualified buyers reach the relevant team. Success means an accurately routed, actually received enquiry, not a clicked button.

## Brand Personality

Precise, composed, accountable. The owner's direction is professional and enterprise-grade, with life and motion but without a generic AI-generated template appearance.

## Anti-references

Owner-rejected generic redesigns, oversized homepage copy, heavy blue backgrounds, tiny logo, repeated generic cards and superficial animation. Do not put customer case-study narratives on the homepage.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve approved business facts and distinguish clients, partners and technologies.
2. Make familiar practice names and contact routes immediately discoverable.
3. Repair shared foundations before decorative redesign.
4. Use motion to support comprehension, with user control and static alternatives.
5. Treat actual delivery and recovery states as part of the design.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA across both themes, keyboard use, reflow/zoom and reduced motion. Default rendering must not depend on JavaScript to reveal meaningful content or choose an initial theme. Do not claim certification based solely on automated checks.
