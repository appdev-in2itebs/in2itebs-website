## 4. AMS transition  `#transition`
<!-- SOT-39 -->
**Headline:** 16 weeks to safe *steady-state*.
**Body:** A five-phase AMS transition from pre-transition planning to full-service commencement, with quality gates at every phase and overlap with incumbent teams.
- **Pre-Transition Planning (Wks -4–0)** — governance, transition plan, connectivity, service-desk bonding.
- **Planning (Wks 1–2)** — onboarding, detailed KT plan, access provisioning.
- **Knowledge Acquisition (Wks 3–7)** — KT sessions, application-understanding and process docs.
- **Shadow (Wks 8–12)** — shadow live tickets, operational readiness review.
