## 1. The big idea behind the whole site

Deck 2 repositions In2IT EBS as **an AI-led enterprise transformation company** — a firm that helps large organisations modernise the core software that runs their business, with artificial intelligence built into everything it does. ("Artificial intelligence," or AI, simply means software that can understand language, spot patterns and make recommendations the way a skilled person would.)

The whole company — and therefore the whole website — is organised around **three pillars plus a delivery layer**:

- **Pillar A — Platform Services:** Setting up and running the big "off-the-shelf" business software platforms (SAP, Salesforce, Workday). *Their phrase: "platform-led transformation."*
- **Pillar B — Advisory Services:** The expert consulting that decides *what* a company should change and *why*, before any building starts. *Their phrase: "consulting-led transformation."*
- **Pillar C — Digital, Data & AI:** Custom-built software, artificial intelligence, and getting value out of company data. *Their phrase: "engineering-led transformation."*
- **A delivery layer — "Delivery Excellence":** The proven method and teams that actually carry out the work across all three pillars.

The promise that ties it together (straight from the deck): **"One Accountable Partner."** Instead of juggling several suppliers, the client deals with In2IT EBS for advice, building, support and ongoing improvement — all under one roof. The deck calls this idea **"Converged Intelligence."**

> **Plain-English note on one technical term you'll see a lot:** **SAP** is the world's most widely used software for running a large company's core operations — its finance, supply chain, HR and more. A great deal of In2IT EBS's work centres on SAP. There's a short glossary at the end for SAP and the other shorthand terms.

## 6. How the writing should sound

- **Confident, editorial, and evidence-led** — aimed at senior buyers (the IT chief, the finance chief, the transformation lead). Use British/international English.
- **Lead with outcomes and the "one accountable partner" idea**, not long lists of features.
- **Every claim is backed by a number or credential from the deck.** Never invent capabilities, clients or figures.
- **Headlines have a distinctive look:** the main words are set in the serif title font, with **one** word picked out in italic accent blue — for example, *"Three paths to the latest SAP — we run all **three**."* This is the editorial signature of the brand.
- **Suggested button wording:** the main one is "Let's talk about your transformation"; on the SAP decision page, "Take the 2-week Pathway Assessment"; on case studies, "Read the case study" (avoid bland words like "Explore" or "View").

## Glossary (plain-English)

- **SAP** — the world's most widely used software for running a large company's core operations (finance, supply chain, HR, etc.). In2IT EBS's biggest specialism.
- **ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning)** — the general term for the kind of all-in-one business software SAP provides; it ties a company's main functions into one system.
- **S/4HANA** — the name of SAP's current, modern generation of its core software. Moving to it is a major project for most companies.
- **RISE** — one route to adopting S/4HANA, suited to larger, more complex organisations that need more customisation.
- **GROW** — another route to adopting S/4HANA, suited to mid-sized or fast-moving organisations that want a quicker, more standardised set-up.
- **PCE (Private Cloud Edition)** — a version of S/4HANA run on a dedicated, private slice of the cloud, for organisations needing deep customisation or strict data isolation.
- **AMS (Application Management Services)** — ongoing support and improvement of software after it has gone live; the "run" stage.
- **BFSI (Banking, Financial Services and Insurance)** — shorthand for the finance industry as a sector.
- **SuccessFactors / HXM (Human Experience Management)** — SAP's software for HR (hiring, pay, learning, performance).
- **Salesforce** — leading software for managing sales, customer service and marketing.
- **Workday** — leading software for HR and finance.
- **Joule** — SAP's built-in artificial-intelligence assistant.
- **Cloud / hyperscalers** — "the cloud" means software running over the internet rather than on a company's own computers; "hyperscalers" are the giant cloud providers: Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud.
- **Migration** — moving a company from old software to new software, including all its data.
- **Greenfield / Brownfield** — a "greenfield" project starts fresh with a clean system; a "brownfield" project upgrades the existing system in place, keeping its history.
- **AI (Artificial Intelligence) / GenAI (Generative AI)** — software that understands language, spots patterns and makes recommendations; "generative" AI can also create text, summaries or images on demand.
- **ABAP** — SAP's own programming language, used to build custom additions to SAP.
- **CMMI / ISO 9001 / ISO 27001** — independent quality and security certifications that signal the company meets recognised international standards.
- **ECC** — the older generation of SAP's core software, which many companies are now upgrading from to S/4HANA.
