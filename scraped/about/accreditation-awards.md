## Quality Management & Certifications

In2IT EBS represents one of the rare success stories in Indian software, committed to continuous process improvement and consistent quality standards. The organization has established a robust "Total Quality Management" culture enabling solutions that surpass client expectations.

### CMMI Level 3

The company achieved Capability Maturity Model Integration (CMMI) Maturity Level 3 from the Software Engineering Institute. This "defined" classification indicates documented standard processes subject to ongoing refinement, ensuring consistency across the organization.

### ISO/IEC 20000-1:2005 (Service Management)

This certification validates In2IT EBS's service management systems through an internationally recognized standard emphasizing consistent customer service delivery. The organization adopted an integrated process approach for effective, cost-efficient service delivery.

### ISO 9001:2008 (Quality Management)

The Bhubaneswar delivery center and support functions hold this internationally recognized certification, demonstrating commitment to comprehensive customer service from initial contact through request completion.

### ISO/IEC 27001:2005 (Information Security)

This globally recognized certification reinforces stringent data security management. It provides assurance that data security, safety, and confidentiality form the foundation of the organization's Business Security Management System.

### STPI Accreditation

In2IT EBS is registered as a 100% export-oriented unit with Software Technology Parks of India, demonstrating public commitment to software quality, process capability, and productivity improvements.

### NASSCOM Membership

Member since December 2013 of India's National Association of Software and Service Companies, the premier trade body and commerce chamber for the IT software and services industry.
