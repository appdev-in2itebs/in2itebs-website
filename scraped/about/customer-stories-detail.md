## Welspun Group — Building a Supportive Culture for Employees to Grow and Develop their Skills

URL: https://in2itebs.com/welspun/

**Client:** Welspun Group (conglomerate — textiles, welded line pipes, infrastructure)
**Scale:** 50+ countries, 26,000 employees

### Challenge
- Multiple HR systems with no direct access to finance or manufacturing data
- Lack of integration between core HR systems and third-party applications
- Time-consuming HR processes involving manual spreadsheet processing
- Minimal visibility of HR data and services for employees

### Solution
SAP® SuccessFactors® Human Experience Management Suite integrated with SAP Business Suite powered by SAP HANA®, implemented by In2IT EBS.

**Modules deployed:**
- **Employee Central** — master data repository capturing the entire employment lifecycle
- **Performance & Goals** — mid-year and annual assessments
- **Compensation** — automated eligibility assessment and payout processing
- **Recruiting, Onboarding & Learning** — end-to-end talent acquisition and development
- **Succession & Development** — career planning and successor attribute capture
- **Job Profile Builder** — auto-populates job postings with competency recommendations

### Results
- 24×7 employee access to single-point HR control
- 800+ spreadsheets eliminated through automation (insurance renewal)
- 99.9% goal-setting and performance-assessment achievement
- 95% process adherence and system usage
- 15% cost savings in HR technology
- Zero manual intervention for fully automated processes
- Automated offboarding compliance processes
- Improved time-to-recruit
- High adoption through bi-annual refresher training
