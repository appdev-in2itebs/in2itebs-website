## Dharmendra Sharma — General Manager

URL: https://in2itebs.com/dharmendra-sharma/

**Bio (verbatim themes):**
Over 22 years in IT and enterprise solutions. Recognized for managing SAP practices, overseeing IT functions, and directing ERP initiatives. Has spearheaded SAP S/4HANA and ECC implementations across multiple sectors in Asia and the Middle East. Core competencies include presales engagement, project delivery, IT strategic planning, and customer account management, with emphasis on advancing digital transformation initiatives within the SAP domain.
