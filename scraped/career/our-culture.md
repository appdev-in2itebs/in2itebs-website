## Our Culture

At In2IT EBS, our performance-oriented culture and responsible approach are the foundations of our success. We strive to maintain a fun and positive work environment, encouraging our employees to enjoy themselves at work and think outside the box. We are firm believers in individual interest and the need to let employees find their own rhythm in the work they do. Truth is work has to be appealing and interest must exist in order to gain maximum quality in one's work force and we have experienced, cultivated, and motivated people to find their calling in the profession and achieve great success. We recognize that our business depends on the creativity, dedication, and performance of our employees. We encourage our employees to focus on achievement through collaboration and innovation.

## Diversely United

At In2IT EBS, we value individuals on a personal level. Our team is composed of employees from many walks of life, bringing a variety of experiences, perspectives, and ideas to the table. In2IT EBS is committed to equality of opportunity, fairness, work and lifestyle, mutual respect, and dignity at work for all associates. In2IT EBS values differences including disability, sexual orientation, race, ethnicity, age, thinking styles, gender, and religion.

## Reasons of Success for In2IT EBS

- **Customer-oriented mindset** driven by our core values: customer focus, inclusion, accountability, integrity, teamwork, innovation, open communication, enthusiasm, and empowerment.
- **Knowledge management** to make the business systems and process documentation available to all our employees and promote continuous learning and knowledge sharing.
- **Recruit, develop, and retain talent** by continuously attracting and coaching associates, providing extensive training programs, offering fair compensation, and effective talent management.
