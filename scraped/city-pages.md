# City-Specific Landing Pages (template)

In2IT EBS publishes a near-identical landing page for each of the following cities. All seven pages share the same template, services list, industries list, footer, and CTA — only the city name in the hero copy changes.

## Shared Template (city = `{CITY}`)

**Page title format:** "In2IT {CITY} - In2IT EBS"

**Hero H1:** "In2IT {CITY}"

**Hero body (verbatim):**
> "In2IT EBS delivers specialized IT infrastructure, application, and security solutions in {CITY}, addressing the specific needs of the BFSI, telecommunications, manufacturing, FMCG, and retail sectors. With expertise in advanced technologies such as RPA, machine learning, blockchain, AI, and IoT, we leverage strategic partnerships and dedicated Centers of Excellence to implement high-impact, industry-focused solutions."
>
> "Globally, In2IT EBS excels in IT consultancy, cloud computing, managed services, and application development. We collaborate with clients to streamline operations, enhance solution efficiency, and deliver measurable, customer-driven results."

**Services-We-Offer block:**
- RISE with SAP
- SAP SuccessFactors
- SAP SAC
- Grow with SAP
- Other Enterprise Solutions
- Data Analytics & AI
- Application Development & Maintenance Services (ADMS)

**Industries-We-Focused block:** the same 14 verticals as the rest of the site.

**Primary CTA:** "Talk to Us" → `/expert-advice/`

**Footer:** identical to the rest of the site — see `site-chrome.md`.
