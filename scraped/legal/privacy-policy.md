# Privacy Policy

URL: https://in2itebs.com/privacy-policy-2/

Protecting individual privacy on the Internet is crucial to the future of Internet-based business and the move toward a true Internet economy. In2IT EBS has created this Privacy Statement to demonstrate commitment to data protection and privacy rights. This Privacy Statement outlines how the organization handles information that can be used to directly or indirectly identify an individual ("Personal Data").

## General Information

### Who is the Data Controller?

The data controller of www.in2itebs.com is In2IT EBS. Where a registration form is presented on this website, the data controller may vary depending on the actual offering or the purpose of data collection, but it is displayed on the individual registration form's privacy statement.

### What Personal Data does In2IT EBS collect?

When you visit In2IT EBS's websites, the organization stores certain information about your browser, operating system, and IP address.

If you use a registration form, the website collects information you provide, which consists of:
- First and last name
- Email addresses
- Telephone numbers
- Location (country, state/province, city)
- Company name
- Job title and role
- Department and function
- Current relationship to In2IT EBS
- Company's industry

### Why does In2IT EBS need your Personal Data?

In2IT EBS requires your Personal Data to:
- Provide you with access to the site
- Deliver any ordered goods or services
- Comply with statutory obligations, including checks required by applicable export laws

Providing Personal Data is voluntary, though In2IT EBS may not be able to perform or satisfy your request without it. You can order goods or services without providing consent into In2IT EBS's further marketing operations.

### From What Types of Third Parties does In2IT EBS obtain Personal Data?

In most cases, In2IT EBS collects Personal Data directly from you. The organization might also obtain Personal Data from third parties if applicable national law allows. These third-party sources include:

- In2IT EBS and/or In2IT EBS Group's business dealings with your employer
- Third parties you directed to share your Personal Data with In2IT EBS

### How long will In2IT EBS store my Personal Data?

In2IT EBS will only store your Personal Data for as long as it is required to:

- Make goods and services requested available to you, including use of In2IT EBS.com
- Comply with statutory obligations resulting from applicable export laws
- Until you object against such use if In2IT EBS's use of your Personal Data is based on In2IT EBS's legitimate business interest
- Until you revoke your consent granted in this Privacy Statement
- Where your Personal Data is required for In2IT EBS to assert or defend against legal claims, until the end of the relevant retention period or until the claims in question have been settled

### Who are the recipients of your Personal Data and where will it be processed?

Your Personal Data will be passed on to the following categories of third parties to process your information:

- Third-party service providers for the provision of the website, newsletter dispatch, consulting services, and other related services

As a global group of companies operating internationally, In2IT EBS has affiliates and third-party service providers outside of the European Economic Area (the "EEA") and will transfer your Personal Data to countries outside of the EEA. If these transfers are to a country for which the EU Commission has not issued an adequacy decision, In2IT EBS uses EU standard contractual clauses to contractually require that your Personal Data receives a level of data protection consistent with the EEA.

### What are your data protection rights?

You can request from In2IT EBS:
- Access at any time to information about which Personal Data In2IT EBS processes about you
- Correction or deletion of such Personal Data

However, In2IT EBS can or will delete your Personal Data only if there is no statutory obligation or prevailing right of In2IT EBS to retain it. If you request that In2IT EBS deletes your Personal Data, you will not be able to continue to use any In2IT EBS service that requires the organization's use of your Personal Data.

If In2IT EBS uses your Personal Data based on your consent or to perform a contract with you, you can further request a copy of the Personal Data that you provided to In2IT EBS. To do so, please contact the email address provided and specify the information or processing activities to which your request relates, the format in which you would like to receive this information, and whether the Personal Data should be sent to you or another recipient.

Furthermore, you can request that In2IT EBS restricts your Personal Data from any further processing in the following events:
- You state that the Personal Data In2IT EBS has about you is incorrect
- There is no legal basis for In2IT EBS processing your Personal Data and you demand restriction
- In2IT EBS no longer requires your Personal Data but you require retention to claim or exercise legal rights or defend against third party claims
- You object to the processing of your Personal Data by In2IT EBS based on In2IT EBS's legitimate interest

#### For individuals within India

You have the right to:
- Request access to your Personal Data that In2IT EBS collects, uses, discloses, or sells (if applicable) about you
- Request that In2IT EBS delete Personal Data about you
- Receive non-discriminatory treatment for exercise of any data protection rights
- Request that information be portable in a readily usable format that allows you to transmit this information to another recipient without hindrance
- Opt-out of the sale of Personal Data

In accordance with disclosure requirements, In2IT EBS is exempt from providing a notice to opt-out because it does not and will not sell your Personal Data.

### How can you exercise your data protection rights?

Please direct any requests to exercise your rights to info@in2itebs.com.

For individuals within India, you can also designate an authorized agent to submit requests to exercise your data protection rights to In2IT EBS.

### How will In2IT EBS verify requests to exercise data protection rights?

In2IT EBS will take steps to ensure verification of your identity to a reasonable degree of certainty before processing the data protection right you want to exercise. When feasible, In2IT EBS will match Personal Data provided by you in submitting a request with information already maintained by the organization.

In accordance with verification processes, In2IT EBS will require a more stringent verification process for deletion requests or for Personal Data that is considered sensitive or valuable. If In2IT EBS must request additional information from you outside of information already maintained, the organization will only use it for the purposes of verifying your identity so you can exercise your data protection rights, or for security and fraud-prevention purposes.

In2IT EBS will decline to process requests that are manifestly unfounded, excessive, fraudulent, or are not otherwise required by local law.

### Right to lodge a complaint

If you take the view that In2IT EBS is not processing your Personal Data in accordance with the requirements in this Privacy Statement or under applicable EEA data protection laws, you can at any time lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of the EEA country where you live or with the data protection authority of the country or state where In2IT EBS has its registered seat.

### Can I use In2IT EBS's goods and services if I am a minor or child?

#### Children

In2IT EBS websites and online services are not directed to users below the age of 16 years, or equivalent minimum age in the relevant jurisdiction. If you are younger than 16, you cannot register with and use these websites or online services.

#### U.S. Children's Privacy

In2IT EBS does not knowingly collect the Personal Data of children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe In2IT EBS collected information about a child, please contact In2IT EBS as described in this Privacy Statement. In2IT EBS will take steps to delete the information as soon as possible. Given that In2IT EBS websites and online services are not directed to users under 16 years of age and in accordance with CCPA disclosure requirements, In2IT EBS does not sell the Personal Data of any minors under 16 years of age.

## Processing based on a statutory permission

IN2IT EBS requires your Personal Data to:

Processing when necessary to fulfill IN2IT EBS's contractual obligation includes:
- Responding to your related inquiries
- Processing your feedback
- Providing you with support
- Conversation data that you may initiate or enable such as through chat functionalities, contact forms, emails, or by telephone

- Send you notice of payments

For marketing-related communications such as emails and phone calls, IN2IT EBS will:
- Only provide you with such information after you have opted in, if legally required
- Provide you the opportunity to opt out if you do not want to receive further marketing-related communications

IN2IT EBS and its products, technologies, and services are subject to the export laws of various countries including, without limitation, those of the European Union and its member states, and of the United States of America. You acknowledge that, pursuant to the applicable export laws, trade sanctions, and embargoes issued by these countries, IN2IT EBS is required to take measures to prevent entities, organizations, and parties listed on government-issued sanctioned-party lists from accessing certain products, technologies, and services through IN2IT EBS's websites or other delivery channels controlled by IN2IT EBS.

Any such use of your Personal Data is based on the permission to process Personal Data in order to comply with statutory obligations (Article 6(1) lit. c GDPR) and IN2IT EBS's legitimate interest (Article 6(1) lit. f GDPR) or under the equivalent articles under other national laws, when applicable.

#### Processing based on IN2IT EBS's legitimate interest

IN2IT EBS can use your Personal Data based on its legitimate interest (Article 6(1), lit. f GDPR) or the equivalent article under other national laws, when applicable, as follows:

##### Fraud and Legal Claims
If required, IN2IT EBS will use your Personal Data for the purposes of preventing or prosecuting criminal activities such as fraud and to assert or defend against legal claims.

##### Questionnaires and surveys
IN2IT EBS could invite you to participate in questionnaires and surveys. These questionnaires and surveys will be generally designed in a way that they can be answered without any data that can be used to identify you. If you nonetheless enter such data in a questionnaire or survey, IN2IT EBS will use this Personal Data to improve its products and services.

##### Contract Performance
If you purchase or intend to purchase goods or services from IN2IT EBS on behalf of a corporate customer or otherwise be the nominated contact person for the business relationship between a corporate customer (a "Customer Contact") and IN2IT EBS, IN2IT EBS will use your Personal Data for this purpose. This includes, for the avoidance of doubt, such steps which are required for establishing the relevant business relationship. In case that an existing Customer Contact informs IN2IT EBS that you are his replacement, IN2IT EBS will, from the point in time of such notification, consider you to be the relevant Customer Contact for the respective customer until you object.

##### Creation of anonymized data sets
IN2IT EBS will anonymize Personal Data provided under this Privacy Statement to create anonymized data sets, which will then be used to improve its and its affiliates' products and services.

##### Personalized Content
If you opt-in to receive marketing communications such as and including newsletters, brochures or white papers from IN2IT EBS, IN2IT EBS will collect and store details of how you interact with such content to help create, develop, operate, deliver and improve communications with you. This information is aggregated and used to help IN2IT EBS provide more useful information and to understand what is of most interest to you.

##### Recordings for quality improvement purposes
In case of telephone calls or chat sessions, IN2IT EBS will record such calls (after informing you accordingly during that call and before the recording starts) or chat sessions to improve the quality of IN2IT EBS's services.

##### To keep you up-to-date or request feedback
Within an existing business relationship between you and IN2IT EBS, the organization might inform you, where permitted in accordance with local laws, about its products or services (including webinars, seminars and events) which are similar or relate to such products and services you have already purchased or used from IN2IT EBS. #### Right to object
You can object to IN2IT EBS's use of your Personal Data as set forth in this section at any time by updating that preference. In this case, IN2IT EBS will carefully review your objection and cease further use of the relevant information unless IN2IT EBS has a compelling legitimate ground for continued use of the information and such ground overrides your objection, or if IN2IT EBS requires the information for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.
