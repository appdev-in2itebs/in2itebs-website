# A Closer Look At Hubspot's 2022 Hybrid Work Report

URL: https://in2itebs.com/live-stream-from-awwwards-showcasing-trends/

- **Author:** Sudhansu Sekhar Mishra — AGM IT Application, In2IT Enterprise Business Services Pvt. Ltd.
- **Date:** 29 March 2022 (listed as "08 Dec" on the news index)

## The COVID Chaos
The COVID-19 pandemic significantly impacted working life. The Hubspot report surveyed four thousand full-time flex, in-office, and remote workers across the US, UK, Germany, Ireland, France, Japan, Australia, and Canada, examining culture, collaboration, communication, management, and mental health.

## Better Flexibility
Employees appreciated working both onsite and remotely. Companies are considering adopting hybrid work models, with the priority in 2022 being to "future-proof that work model."

## Collaboration And Communication Present More Challenges
Hybrid workforces face collaboration obstacles. Nearly half of remote workers missed "in-person, spontaneous connections" with colleagues. Approximately 60% of office-based workers found connecting with teams and staying motivated challenging. Meanwhile, 61% of flexible workers reported effective team relationships.

> "Almost 40% of respondents said they'd prefer to go to the dentist every month than go to the office five days each week!"

## Employee Experience Helps Culture Thrive
20% of flexible workers identified differences between remote and in-office experiences as obstacles. 60% ranked better work/life balance as their top cultural priority. Nearly half preferred an extra day off over increased salary; 31% valued anonymous feedback opportunities.

## Better Communication For Better Long-Term Productivity
34% of flexible workers visited offices for better colleague communication.

> "72% of respondents said that too many meetings and calls in the workplace disrupted their concentration."

## Holistic Approaches to Mental Health
40% experienced improved work/life balance, yet nearly 60% struggled setting boundaries. Parents and caregivers particularly felt pressure to remain "permanently on."

## Better Resources For Team Support
Nearly 30% of flexible workers found managing remote colleagues challenging. Almost half of managers cited communication breakdowns as top concerns. 60% believed they had appropriate tools for effective hybrid management.
