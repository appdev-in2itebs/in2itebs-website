# Coronavirus lockdown leads to more gaming and e-Sports

URL: https://in2itebs.com/coronavirus-lockdown-leads-to-more-gaming-and-e-sports/
Date: 21 Jun

The esports and gaming sector emerged as an unexpected beneficiary during China's coronavirus lockdown, with approximately 45 million people confined to their homes. "The Chinese are immense gamers and love their esports," and China represents the world's largest game streaming market, boasting roughly 4.9 times the monthly active users of the U.S. market in 2018.

Confinement to homes increased online spending, potentially boosting China's gaming and esports sector in the first half of 2020. Key investment opportunities included:

**Tencent** – China's leading esports game streaming and online gaming company, which acquired Riot Games and operates the highly popular League of Legends. Tencent also owns DouYu, China's second-ranked game streaming platform, which had 159.2 million monthly active users in Q1 2019.

**Huya** – Positioned as "the Twitch of China," Huya ranks as China's top game streaming platform.

**NERD and ESPO ETFs** – For those preferring esports exchange-traded funds, these two options offer broad global esports exposure.

**Enthusiast Gaming Holdings Inc.** – Among the fastest-growing esports organizations globally and one of few publicly-traded pure-play esports companies, it operates "the biggest gaming network in North America and the English talking gaming districts," reaching over 200 million monthly gamers.

**Atari** – The legendary gaming company is opening branded gaming inns across eight U.S. cities featuring virtual reality, augmented reality, online game studios, and esports event venues.
