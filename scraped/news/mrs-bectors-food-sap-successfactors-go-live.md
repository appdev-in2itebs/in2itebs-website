# Mrs Bectors Food-SAP SuccessFactors Go-Live partnering with In2IT EBS

URL: https://in2itebs.com/future-where-technology-creates-good-jobs/
Date: 08 Dec (published context: 9th May 2022)
Author: Dr. Sanjeev Kumarr Dixit, CHRO – Mrs. Bectors Food Specialities Ltd.

May 9th, 2022 represents a significant milestone for the MBFSL team as the organization undertakes "LEAP (Leveraging Effectiveness thru Automating Processes)" — a transition from manual operations to a cloud-based Human Capital Management system using SAP SuccessFactors.

The implementation encompasses comprehensive employee data management, including self-information, employee master data, organizational structures, and leave management processes. The GROW Performance Management system will handle KRA setting, monthly target reviews, annual performance evaluations including self-assessments, manager evaluations, and HR calibration — all accessible through login credentials provided during onboarding training.

From May 9th through May 14th, designated as "LEAP WEEK," In2IT's implementation team will conduct user training sessions and distribute self-help manuals. HR team members and managers will provide support to ensure effective system adoption and organizational productivity gains.

Dr. Dixit acknowledged the HR team's dedication in completing the project within four months and commended In2IT for their collaborative project management and expert contributions. While acknowledging potential initial challenges common to new system implementations, he emphasized the team's collective commitment to transforming obstacles into learning opportunities.

The message concludes with an encouraging call to embrace the system's benefits.
