## RISE WITH SAP

### SAP S/4HANA

SAP S/4HANA is a real-time enterprise resource management suite for digital business. It is built on the advanced in-memory platform SAP HANA, and offers a personalized, consumer-grade user experience with SAP Fiori. It is deployable in on-premise, cloud, and hybrid environments.

SAP S/4HANA is designed for enterprises across industries that need deep and broad functionality combined with high customization flexibility.

SAP S/4HANA serves as the digital core for your business. Built on the SAP HANA platform, it enables the digital network connecting your entire workforce, suppliers, customers, and assets to the digital core.

- Provides industry-leading capabilities across 25+ industries with unmatched depth
