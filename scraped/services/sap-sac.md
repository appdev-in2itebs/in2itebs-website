# SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC)

URL: https://in2itebs.com/sap-sac/

**SAP Analytics Cloud** is a cloud-based solution combining business intelligence, enterprise planning, and augmented analytics in a single offering. Built on SAP Business Technology Platform, it enables organizations to eliminate point solutions and break down data silos.

## Overview

Rather than relying on standalone spreadsheets or disconnected reporting tools, users have everything needed to make fast, confident decisions together. The platform supports the continuous intelligent decision-making process: plan, discover, analyze, predict, and act.

### Key Advantages

- Make end-to-end decisions with all analytics capabilities in one place
- Gain enterprise-ready insight and act within your business processes
- Make smart decisions with AI-driven insights delivered naturally

Organizations benefit from:

- Complete, comprehensive, in-memory data management and analytics
- Contextual delivery of smart insights through natural-language processing
- Confident decision-making faster, without expert intervention
- Affordability, availability, and scalability

## Augmented Analytics

Reveal relevant, accurate, and actionable insights with artificial intelligence and machine learning:

- **Data science for all** – Focus on higher-value activities with embedded machine intelligence
- **Conversational analytics** – Get answers instantly with natural language querying
- **Automated machine learning** – Discover key influencers and detect anomalies
- **Intelligent predictions** – Predict outcomes and forecasts without data science skills

## Business Intelligence

Explore data across the organization with intuitive self-service analytics:

- **Data exploration and visualization** – Create interactive visualizations and stories
- **Efficient modeling and preparation** – Automate data cleansing and categorization

## Enterprise Planning

Link financial and operational plans seamlessly:

- **Collaborative enterprise planning** – Plan across all business lines
- **Intelligent planning** – Use predictive forecasting and machine learning
- **Integrated financial planning** – Combine transactions, analytics, and planning with SAP S/4HANA
- **Company-wide advanced analytics** – Budget, forecast, and analyze from one cloud interface

## Design of Analytics Applications

Create centrally governable analytic content:

Access analytics anytime, anywhere:
