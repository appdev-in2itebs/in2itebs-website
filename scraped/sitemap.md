## Duplicate / alias URLs

Many service, industry, and partner pages have both a canonical slug and a `-2` slug (e.g. `/fmcg/` ↔ `/fmcg-2/`, `/sap-concur/` ↔ `/sap-concur-2/`). The page-sitemap currently lists the `-2` variants alongside the canonicals; spot-checking shows them serving the same content.
