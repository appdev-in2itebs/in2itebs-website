# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Enterprise technology leaders, transformation sponsors, procurement teams, and functional leaders evaluating complex SAP-led and adjacent platform programmes. Secondary users include partners, candidates, and existing clients looking for capability or contact information.

## Product Purpose

Explain In2IT EBS clearly, establish delivery credibility, connect capabilities to relevant proof, and move qualified visitors toward a focused 30-minute discovery workshop.

## Brand Personality

Assured, exact, globally capable. The experience should feel calm under complexity, specific about outcomes, and confident without becoming loud or theatrical.

## Anti-references

- Generic consulting templates built from repeated card grids.
- Dense procurement catalogues that expose the full taxonomy at once.
- Abstract AI decoration that competes with business evidence.
- Auto-moving proof, hidden controls, and interactions that depend on discovery.
- Editorial styling used as decoration rather than hierarchy.

## Design Principles

1. Clarity before coverage. Reveal depth progressively instead of presenting the full service catalogue at once.
2. Proof beside the claim. Put measurable outcomes and named evidence where confidence is requested.
3. One obvious next step. Keep a single primary conversion action and make secondary navigation clearly secondary.
4. Visible affordances. Interactive elements must look interactive and respond immediately.
5. Calm control. Use motion, color, and density to reduce uncertainty, not manufacture excitement.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA for normal text, large text, controls, focus indicators, and both light and dark themes. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, descriptive labels, 44px minimum touch targets, and content that remains accurate without animation or client-side JavaScript.
